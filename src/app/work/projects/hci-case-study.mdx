## Overview

This project is a detailed Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) case study that emphasizes user-centered design processes. It demonstrates the complete lifecycle of interface design, starting from raw ideation and low-fidelity sketching, through to the creation of interactive, high-fidelity prototypes.

## Process and Methodology

- **User Research:** Identifying pain points and building empathy with the target audience.
- **Low-Fidelity Wireframes:** Rapid sketching to establish the foundational layout and user flow without getting bogged down by aesthetic details.
- **High-Fidelity Prototypes:** Implementing visual design principles, typography, and interactive modern components to simulate the final product.
- **Usability Testing:** Evaluating the designs with real users to refine the experience.

## Tech Stack & Tools

- **Design:** Figma, Canva
- **Concepts:** HCI Principles, Usability Heuristics, User-Centered Design (UCD)

This case study sharpened my ability to think from the user's perspective while balancing functional requirements with aesthetic appeal, proving that a great product is built on a foundation of empathy and iteration.
